# DiscountIQ — Smart Discount and Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
DiscountIQ is a data analytics project that evaluates how discounts affect sales, revenue, and profitability. It aims to identify unprofitable discount strategies and recommend better discount decisions using data-driven insights.

## Business Problem
Excessive discounts may increase sales but reduce profit margins or cause losses. Businesses need to understand which discount levels are profitable across product categories, regions, and sales channels.

## Tools Used
- SQL — Business analysis and querying
- Python (Pandas) — Data cleaning and analysis
- Power BI — Interactive dashboard and visualization

## Dataset
- 25,000 transaction records
- Period: January 2023 – August 2024
- Product categories: Grocery, Home & Kitchen, Apparel, Electronics, Sports, and Beauty
- Regions: East, West, North, and South
- Sales channels: Store and Online

## Key Analysis
- Revenue and profit performance
- Discount percentage and profit margin analysis
- Loss-making transactions
- Category-wise and region-wise profitability
- Sales channel performance
- Identification of discount levels that affect profitability

## Recommendations
- Identify discount levels that maintain profitability rather than focusing only on sales growth.
- Review high-discount transactions that generate low profits or losses.
- Set category-specific discount limits based on cost and margin.
- Compare regional and online versus store performance before adjusting discount strategies.
- Monitor revenue, profit, and margin through a Power BI dashboard.
- Review discount performance regularly and adjust strategies based on business results.

## Project Outcome
The project provides a structured approach to discount and profitability analysis, helping businesses make informed pricing decisions and reduce the risk of margin erosion.
